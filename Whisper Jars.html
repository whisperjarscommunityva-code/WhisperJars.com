🌱 Our Mission

Rooted in community,
growing together

Whisper Jars began as a simple idea: what if every student in Northern Virginia could start their day with a message of encouragement — from someone just like them?

By partnering with local schools and libraries, we are creating a regional network of positivity. Our affirmation jars, workshops, and toolkits give educators practical tools to foster healthy mindsets — at no cost.

We welcome every county, every classroom, every voice. The more notes in the jar, the stronger our community becomes.
